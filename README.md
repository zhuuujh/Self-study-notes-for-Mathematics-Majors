# Self-learning-notes-for-Mathematics-Majors

# 高等代数 Advanced Algebra

《高等代数》为哈工大工科必修基础课《代数与几何》的代数部分延伸课程，以结构化数学语言阐释代数结构为核心教学目标。利用B站公开视频资源 https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yfWLzhEeJ 完成学习：从直观的角度切入，探究幂等变换、幂零变换的理论特征，分析二者矩阵特性并对接若尔当块与若尔当标准形；专项研讨广义逆矩阵的由来，性质与工程意义；由柯西-施瓦茨不等式引出Gram矩阵，梳理其几何意义；最后详细分析辛代数结构，包括辛变换、辛矩阵、辛群，并对比其与欧氏空间、酉空间的关联。<br>

As an extended algebra course of *Algebra and Geometry*, a compulsory foundational subject for engineering majors at Harbin Institute of Technology, Advanced Algebra centers on interpreting various algebraic structures through systematic and standardized mathematical language. This study is carried out systematically with the support of a teaching video on Bilibili ( https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yfWLzhEeJ ). Starting from an intuitive perspective, we first investigate the theoretical characteristics of idempotent transformations and nilpotent transformations, analyze the inherent properties of their corresponding matrices, and establish the internal connections between such matrices, Jordan blocks and Jordan canonical forms. Afterwards, a special topic is conducted to thoroughly discuss the derivation origin, core properties and engineering application value of generalized inverse matrices. Next, the Gram matrix is introduced via the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, and its geometric connotation is fully sorted out. Finally, we conduct an in-depth analysis of the symplectic algebraic system, elaborate systematically on symplectic transformations, symplectic matrices and symplectic groups, and illustrate the connections and distinctions between symplectic spaces, Euclidean spaces and unitary spaces through comparative analysis.<br>

# 解析几何 Analytic Geometry

《解析几何》为哈工大工科必修基础课《代数与几何》的几何部分延伸课程，以坐标与向量为工具、用代数方法系统刻画几何对象与几何变换为核心教学目标。利用 B 站公开视频资源 https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Ui421a7fw ，配套丘维声《解析几何》教材完成学习：从向量代数与空间直角坐标系切入，建立点、向量与坐标的对应关系；依次研究平面与空间直线的方程及位置关系，推导柱面、锥面、旋转面与五类二次曲面的方程及几何特征；随后进入二次曲线一般理论，系统梳理中心、渐近方向、直径、共轭直径与切线的代数刻画及几何意义；最后探讨正交变换、仿射变换对几何图形的作用，建立度量性质与仿射性质的区分。<br>

As an extended geometry course of *Algebra and Geometry*, a compulsory foundational subject for engineering majors at Harbin Institute of Technology, Analytic Geometry centers on systematically characterizing geometric objects and transformations by algebraic methods, with coordinates and vectors as the fundamental tools. This study is carried out with the support of a public video collection on Bilibili( https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Ui421a7fw ), accompanied by the textbook *Analytic Geometry* by Qiu Weisheng. Starting from vector algebra and the spatial rectangular coordinate system, we establish the correspondence among points, vectors and coordinates. We then investigate in turn the equations and positional relations of planes and spatial lines, and derive the equations and geometric features of cylindrical surfaces, conical surfaces, surfaces of revolution and the five classes of quadric surfaces. Afterwards, we move to the general theory of quadratic curves, systematically sorting out the algebraic characterization and geometric significance of centers, asymptotic directions, diameters, conjugate diameters and tangent lines. Finally, we discuss the effects of orthogonal transformations and affine transformations on geometric figures, establishing the distinction between metric properties and affine properties.<br>

# 抽象代数 Abstract Algebra

《抽象代数》为哈工大工科必修基础课《离散数学》的进阶课程，以公理化体系研究基本代数结构为核心教学目标。依托 B 站公开视频资源 https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1H2NoeiE1K 学习：从运算公理切入，依次探究群的基础性质、循环群、子群、陪集与拉格朗日定理，深入正规子群、商群、可解群、半直积与群的线性表示；随后拓展至环论，讲解理想、唯一分解整环 UFD、主理想整环 PID；进一步研讨有限域、有序域与代数闭域等各类域结构；最后引入模、对偶模、自由模，并借助交换图与泛性质搭建范畴视角下的代数统一框架。<br>

As an advanced extension of Discrete Mathematics, a compulsory foundational course for engineering students at Harbin Institute of Technology, Abstract Algebra centers on studying fundamental algebraic structures via an axiomatic system. I completed self-study relying on open Bilibili lecture resources: [https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1H2NoeiE1K]. Starting from operation axioms, the course sequentially explores basic properties of groups, cyclic groups, subgroups, cosets and Lagrange’s Theorem, then delves deep into normal subgroups, quotient groups, solvable groups, semidirect products and linear representations of groups. It later expands to ring theory, covering ideals, Unique Factorization Domains (UFDs) and Principal Ideal Domains (PIDs). The curriculum further investigates various field structures including finite fields, ordered fields and algebraically closed fields. Finally, modules, dual modules and free modules are introduced, and a unified algebraic framework from a categorical perspective is constructed through commutative diagrams and universal properties.<br>
